Frequently Asked Questions

What should I wear during my therapy?

We provide high-grade disposable spa undergarments, fresh towels, and plush robes. Therapists practice professional draping techniques, exposing only the specific area being massaged. For Thai Yoga bodywork, loose comfortable attire is provided.

Are therapists certified and verified?

Yes, 100%. All therapists at Glow Spa are certified graduates of accredited wellness academies with documented professional credentials and police background verifications. They maintain respectful decorum at all times.

Is advance booking mandatory, or can I walk in?

While walk-ins are welcomed based on room availability, we strongly recommend booking 1–2 hours ahead via WhatsApp to guarantee your preferred suite, AC setting, and therapist choice.
